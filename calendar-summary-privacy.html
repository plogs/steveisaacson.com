Personal Calendar Summary · Steve Isaacson

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Personal Calendar Summary is a private calendar-summary application operated by Steve Isaacson. This policy explains how the application accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when a user authorizes access to Google Calendar.

This policy applies to Personal Calendar Summary. It does not describe unrelated content or services on steveisaacson.com.

Information the application accesses

With your authorization, the application uses read-only Google Calendar access to retrieve your accessible calendar list and events for today, the current week, and the following week. Accessible calendars may include shared or subscribed calendars available to your Google account.

Information processed includes calendar names and identifiers; event titles, dates, start and end times, locations, descriptions; and other event metadata returned by Google Calendar. The application also uses the email address configured to receive the summary.

Google provides OAuth access and refresh tokens so the application can continue authorized access without asking you to sign in each day. The application does not receive your Google password and does not create, change, or delete your Google Calendar events.

How the information is used

The application uses calendar information to prepare a daily email containing today's summary and listings for this week and next week. Calendar information is used to provide this feature, operate the application, and address technical or security problems.

OpenAI processing and email delivery

To generate the daily summary, the application sends today's event information to OpenAI through its API. This includes calendar names or identifiers used as display labels, event titles, start and end dates or times, locations, and descriptions. Google's authorization tokens are not included in the text sent to OpenAI.

OpenAI processes that information to generate the requested summary. Its handling and retention of API requests and responses depend on the applicable API service terms and account settings. Authorizing calendar access for this application should be accompanied by your informed agreement to this summary processing.

The resulting summary and weekly listings are sent to the configured recipient through email systems. Those systems and the recipient's mailbox process and may retain the message. The application does not publish calendar information on this website.

Limits on use and sharing

Google Calendar information is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. Transfers are limited to providing the requested summary through the disclosed processing and email services, necessary security purposes, or compliance with applicable law.

Personal Calendar Summary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention, and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's server for continued authorized access. The application reads calendar event data while preparing each summary rather than maintaining a separate calendar database. Generated email messages, delivery queues, operational logs, and backups may retain information according to the retention settings of those systems.

The operator limits access to stored credentials and uses HTTPS for Google and OpenAI API connections. Email security depends on the systems used to deliver and store messages. No system can guarantee complete security.

Credentials are retained while the calendar connection remains in use. You may request deletion of stored credentials and identifiable application data under the operator's control. Copies in recipients' mailboxes must be deleted by those recipients; service-provider records are governed by the applicable provider's retention practices.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop future Google Calendar access by removing the application's connection from your Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access does not delete summaries already delivered by email.

To stop summary emails, request deletion, or ask about data handling, contact Steve Isaacson at stevei@plnkx.org. The operator will confirm the account or information involved before acting on a request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or its data practices. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Material changes involving additional data access or new processing purposes will be explained before that processing begins, with any necessary consent obtained.
